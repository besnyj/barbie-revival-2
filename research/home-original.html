Barbie - Fun Games for Girls, Videos & Activities

Calling all fab dolls! Join Barbie and friends and play games for girls, watch Barbie Videos, and dress up with the glam-tastic Barbie fashion. Enjoy a wide variety of activities for girls, including dress up games, makeover activities, hair styling and hair salon games. Hang out in the Barbie Bedroom or with Barbie Sisters and Pets online and think pink!

Barbie loves to spend time in her bedroom, where she listens and dances to music, emails friends, and watches her favorite Barbie videos online. When she's not in her room, she often browses through her beautiful walk-in closet. From super stylish shoes, to chic hair accessories, to glitter-ized fashions, you’ll have a blast with Barbie dress up.

On sunny days, Barbie enjoys taking a walk through her lovely Magic Garden. Filled with Barbie princess games, fairy secrets and surprises, it's easy to see why the Garden is so enchanting! You'll learn how to be 'fairy fabulous' and 'princess perfect' in this magical land.

Whether you're playing fun games, watching videos, or hanging out with Barbie in her room, closet or garden, there is plenty to do at Barbie.com. Get fashion advice and beauty tips, and offer your own style suggestions to keep Barbie looking her best. Let’s have some fun!
