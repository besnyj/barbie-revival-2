The Barbie Magic Garden: Princess and Fairy Games for Girls

Take a stroll through the beautiful Magic Garden with Barbie and friends. Games from the Princess Charm School and A Mermaid Tale 2 movie to magical fairy lands and family holiday fun, the garden is filled with enchanting activities! You can play princess dress-up games, learn fairy secrets and even throw Barbie a birthday party amid the beautiful flowers and landscape.

Meet Barbie in the Magic Garden and celebrate its beauty and wonders together. Enter the Princess Palace and watch Barbie videos, solve fun-tastic puzzles and games, find Barbie coloring pages and play princess makeover games. Put on your princess crown and become a beautiful Barbie princess when you visit the Barbie Magic Garden.

Be "fairy fabulous" in Barbie Fashion Fairytale, and play fairy games for girls. In the Magic Garden, you'll participate in secret adventures with exciting surprises. After you have had a fun-filled day outdoors, head inside and hang out in her fabulous bedroom and walk-in closet. There's never a boring moment when you're playing with Barbie!
